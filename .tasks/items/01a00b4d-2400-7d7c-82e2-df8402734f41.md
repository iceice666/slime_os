# `RIGHT_ALL` had two definitions, and the wider one admitted an undefined rights bit

## Context

Resolved 2026-08-17. **Class:** Defect (admission accepted what no contract defined).

the valid capability-rights set was computed two ways — an enumerated union in the builder, a bit-width mask `(1 << 26) - 1` in the root and its checker — and the two disagreed by exactly one undefined bit, which admission's wider mask accepted even though no fixture could produce it.

Class: Defect (admission accepted what no contract defined).
