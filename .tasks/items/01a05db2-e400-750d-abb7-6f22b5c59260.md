# seL4 and `slime-root` on the H1V1

## Context

Complete, observed on the named board on 2026-09-02. The H1V1 boots the seL4 kernel loader, the kernel, and `slime-root` from SD through its unmodified vendor U-Boot, admits only the `aarch64-sel4-nt98690-h1v1` generation, delivers CNTP interrupts before and after graph activation, runs the architecture-neutral sample plane three times with byte-identical normalized traces and zero framing errors, and resets itself through the SoC watchdog after every run. It claims the AArch64 architecture, root, timer, and sample-plane behaviour exercised here and nothing else: storage, network, display, and UART input on this board remain unclaimed. Evidence: [the P6.B devlog entry](../devlog/2026-09-02-p6b-sel4-nt98690-h1v1/index.md). P6.A's observed facts select the kernel configuration: `KernelArmCortexA73` takes 40-bit physical addresses, `MAX_IRQ` is 352 over GICv2 with no system register interface, `TIMER_FREQUENCY` is 12000000 read from `CNTFRQ_EL0` rather than pinned, and the loader is entered at EL2. Work record: [the P6.B devlog entry](../devlog/2026-09-02-p6b-sel4-nt98690-h1v1/index.md).

## Exit conditions

on 2026-09-02 the named H1V1 loaded the 1,285,200-byte sample-plane image from SD in 66 ms, the loader placed the kernel at `0x10000000` and entered it, seL4 dropped to userspace, and `slime-root` reported `untypeds=26 bytes=798838800`, `SLIME_TIMER acquired irq=30 freq_hz=12000000`, the generation admitted, the graph activated with timer delivery live after it, and `SLIME_ROOT READY target_profile=aarch64-sel4-nt98690-h1v1`; the root's `reset request kind=wdt` was followed by the vendor U-Boot banner on all three runs. Raw transcripts are 120,204 bytes each and the normalized traces 9,448 bytes each, byte-identical, with 0 framing errors on a local tty.
