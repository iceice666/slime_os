# Migrate roadmap identity to MyQue work items

## Context

Work-item identity lived in Markdown headings under `roadmap/`, which made the
heading text a foreign key: 288 devlog entries referenced 240 distinct ids, and
`check-devlog.py` established identity by scraping headings and carrying a
hard-coded exception for `B29` and `B30`, each of which had been allocated
twice. Two branches adding backlog defects also always conflicted, because all
94 lived in one 105 KB file.

## Scope

- One MyQue `work-item/v1` item per roadmap declaration, under a canonical
  UUIDv7 in `.tasks/items/`.
- A committed legacy-id map so pre-migration devlog references keep resolving
  without roadmap headings staying authoritative.
- `check-devlog.py` resolves references against the store; its roadmap-heading
  identity machinery is deleted.
- `roadmap/` keeps its problem statements and sequencing as documentation and
  allocates no identity.

Not in scope: rewriting merged devlog front matter, deleting roadmap prose, or
adding SlimeOS-specific fields to `work-item/v1`.

## Exit conditions

- `just tasks_check` and `just devlog_check` both pass, with the devlog's
  288-entry baseline intact and no merged entry body modified.
- No checker derives identity from a roadmap heading, and `B29`/`B30` need no
  identity-level collision exception.
- A reference resolves by UUID; an unresolvable or undetermined-ambiguous
  reference fails the gate.

## Verification

Gates: `just tasks_check`, `just devlog_check`, `just ruff`, `just typos`

## Notes

`.tasks/legacy-roadmap-ids.json` is committed data, not a parse of
`roadmap/*.md`. Nothing new should be added to it: new references are UUIDs.
