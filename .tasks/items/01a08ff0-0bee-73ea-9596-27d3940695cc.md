# Ethernet on the H1V1: declared Synopsys EQoS MAC, uncached-DMA driver, and the TCP plane observed on the board

## Context

The H1V1's Ethernet is a Synopsys DesignWare EQoS MAC (`eth0@2,f04a0000`, compatible
`nvt,synopsys_eth`, 64 KiB of registers at `0x2_f04a_0000`, nine GIC SPIs, `clk_eth0` at
250 MHz, pinmux group `eth`). Nothing in the tree drives it, and the seL4 device tree for the
board carries no node for it. A driver needs the declared-device authority IO8 adds, explicit
cache maintenance because the root maps DMA memory uncached while the MAC's descriptor rings
assume coherent memory, and likely the first real interrupt path, since today's drivers poll.

Sessions follow the P6 pattern: a bench probe from the vendor prompt, a general driver
serving `contracts/link-device/v1` unchanged (QEMU proves "device absent, health green"),
then the board carve, clocks, pinmux, and an observed TCP echo through the host's port.

## Exit conditions

The `sel4-io-tcp` byte stream is observed on the named board through the SoC MAC, with the
responder on the host's Ethernet port and every marker in order.
