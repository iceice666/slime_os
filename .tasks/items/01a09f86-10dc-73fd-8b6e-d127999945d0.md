# Stop requiring devlogs for ordinary changes

## Problem

Active agent, contribution, tutorial, and PR guidance requires a new devlog for
ordinary non-trivial changes and milestone completion. That duplicates the
change record across PRs, commits, work items, owning docs, and a chronological
entry, while obscuring the evidence requirement that actually controls task
closure.

## Scope

- Update every active policy surface together: `AGENTS.md` (and therefore its
  `CLAUDE.md` symlink), `CONTRIBUTING.md`, the first-change tutorial, root/docs
  navigation, PR templates, [`devlog/README.md`](https://git.justaslime.dev/iceice666/slime_os-history/src/commit/45ed1745907b2d0a13fdf70c8b34eb635bed5f23/devlog/README.md), [`devlog/TEMPLATE.md`](https://git.justaslime.dev/iceice666/slime_os-history/src/commit/45ed1745907b2d0a13fdf70c8b34eb635bed5f23/devlog/TEMPLATE.md), and any
  applicable repository-local agent skill discovered at implementation time.
- Ordinary features and bug fixes record change, claim, risk, review surface,
  and exact verification in commits/PRs and the canonical work item; they do
  not require a second chronological write-up.
- Changes to current contracts, operation, or limitations update the owning
  docs. Long-lived cross-module choices use `docs/decisions/`; local invariants
  stay beside code. Expensive reusable exploration or unusual qualification
  campaigns may produce an investigation record.
- A decision records at least context, decision, alternatives/trade-offs,
  consequences, revisit conditions, and relevant UUID/code references. Preserve
  `proposed`, `accepted`, and `superseded`; never promote an old proposal by
  moving or renaming it.
- Keep task closure strict: observe every item-specific exit condition, record
  evidence and its scope/limits in the item, then close it. Removing a mandatory
  devlog does not remove evidence, physical-target, failure-control, or
  fail-closed requirements.
- Leave all existing devlog entries and raw evidence bytes in place. Keep
  [`just devlog_check`](https://git.justaslime.dev/iceice666/slime_os-history/src/commit/45ed1745907b2d0a13fdf70c8b34eb635bed5f23/just/quality.just) meaningful for the legacy corpus until the H3 archive
  cutover; do not replace it with a no-op or unconditional pass.

## Exit conditions

1. A normal non-trivial feature or bug-fix PR can satisfy every active policy
   without adding a devlog entry.
2. No active guide, tutorial, template, or skill states the opposite.
3. PR/work-item evidence requirements remain sufficient to distinguish direct
   observations, inherited evidence, unobserved inference, target/image
   identity, and limitations.
4. Closing a work item still requires its actual exit conditions to be observed
   and recorded; QEMU evidence cannot satisfy a physical target.
5. Existing devlog entries, links, front matter, index, and raw siblings remain
   readable and continue to pass their existing checker.
6. Current documentation directs stable subsystem knowledge to owning docs,
   important decisions to `docs/decisions/`, unfinished designs to
   `docs/plans/`, exploration to `docs/directions/`, and exceptional reusable
   investigations to the history policy defined for the later cutover.
7. Regression coverage proves no new devlog is required while invalid active
   local links/fragments and UUID references still fail.

## Non-goals

Do not move or rewrite historical entries, weaken MyQue/store checks, change
runtime behavior, create a fixed eight-field decision format, require one
decision per PR, or declare the history repository available.
