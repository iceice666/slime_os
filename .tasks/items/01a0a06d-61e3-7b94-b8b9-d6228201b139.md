# The closure aggregate gate cannot see a composed image name or a composed plane flag

## Context

`check-system-image-aggregate.py` learns what a checker boots, and which plane
build flag a checker reaches, by scanning `scripts/check/` for literal strings.
Two changes moved that composition out of the scanned text, and each left the
gate asserting less than it reports.

`0361bff4` (#61) moved image-name composition into `scripts/lib/sel4_boot.py`
(`artifact_paths(stem, platform)`). The product root-boot RV64 image became
invisible to the scan and its own exemption therefore "stale", so the gate
refuses the tree with
`exemption(s) naming an image no checker boots: ['slime-sel4-qemu-riscv-virt.elf']`.
Behind that refusal, the four pc99 images #61 added, its `SLIME_PC99_COM1_PORT`
knob, and the four gates it gave a legacy pc99 arm were all unclassified.

`#123` then bound the capacity arm's flag to a variable and composed it as
`f"--{variant}-plane"` in `check-sel4-private-memory-plane.py`, so
`--private-memory-1g-plane` and `--private-memory-isolation-plane` are reached
by a just target but named by no literal the scan sees, and the gate reports
them as orphans.

#61's other half — `generate-system-test-runs.py` refusing
`check-sel4-x86-64-image.py` because it boots no image — was fixed separately
and is already on `main`; `NOT_A_PLANE` lists that checker there.

## Exit conditions

`just system_image_closure_aggregate_check` and `just contracts_check` pass on
`main`, the gate still refuses an invented plane flag that no target reaches,
and its own named drift controls still refuse their mutations.

## Closure

Closed 2026-09-22 on the observed exit. On `main` at `6168c05`, before the
change, the gate refused with
`exemption(s) naming an image no checker boots: ['slime-sel4-qemu-riscv-virt.elf']`;
after the scan was taught `artifact_paths`, it refused with
`plane flag(s) no just target reaches: ['--private-memory-1g-plane', '--private-memory-isolation-plane']`;
after the composed-flag expansion, it passes with 23 booted plane images
(10 mapped to a closure, 13 exempt with a reason), all 76 closures exercised,
9 plane build flags each reachable from a just target, and 6 named drift
controls refused.

Each added exemption, knob classification, and dual-path entry was re-checked
in place by deleting it and observing the gate refuse, so none is carried
forward as a redundancy already covered on `main`. A flag invented in
`build-sel4.py` and reached by nothing is still reported as an orphan.

The expansion keeps the gate's existing file-level ownership granularity: a
checker's flags are credited to every just target that runs that checker, as
the literal scan already did, so removing one of a checker's several targets is
not reported. Narrowing ownership to the arm is a separate change.
