# Plane gates boot the installed seL4 prefix without comparing it to its observed hashes

## Context

`scripts/check/check-sel4-pins.py` compares the installed prefix's `kernel.elf`, both
`gen_config.json` files, the DTB, and `platform_gen.yaml` against `[observed_prefix]` only
under `--prefix`, and no Justfile target passes it: `just sel4_pin_check`, which every plane
gate depends on, verifies the pinned sources and host tools alone. A prefix built before
`sel4/config/` or the pins moved therefore passes the gate graph, and every plane boots the
previous kernel.

Observed 2026-09-13 on a checkout whose `build/sel4-prefix` dated from 2026-09-02:
`fc86cc7d` had raised `KernelRootCNodeSizeBits` from 12 to 19 and re-blessed
`[observed_prefix]`, `just sel4_pin_check` still reported every pin verified, `just
io_tcp_check`, `just io_network_check`, and `just io_link_check` passed on the 12-bit
kernel, and `just test_sel4_root` was the only gate to refuse, at const-evaluation
(`extent table cannot hold the widest admitted private-extent population`), because
`slime-root` sizes its descriptor tables from the prefix's generated config. Rebuilding with
`just sel4_qemu_image_check` restored the pinned kernel and every gate passed. CI is unaffected
since it builds the prefix fresh; local evidence is what the gap weakens.

## Exit conditions

A gate that boots an image from `build/sel4-prefix` refuses a prefix whose artifacts do not
match the platform's `[observed_prefix]` section, naming the rebuild target, before QEMU is
started; observed by aging one prefix artifact deliberately and reading the refusal from a
plane gate, then rebuilding and reading the pass. `just sel4_gate_control_check` pins the
refusal if the mechanism is a marker or an identity case.

## Verification

Gates: `just sel4_pin_check`, one plane gate such as `just io_link_check` on a stale and a
rebuilt prefix, `just sel4_gate_control_check`.

## Disposition

Deferred 2026-09-13 by decision: the fix changes what the gate graph requires of a checkout
(the prefix comparison must not bind the checkout-only CI job that runs `sel4_pin_check`
without a prefix), so it is its own change with its own Defect entry rather than a rider on
the disk-use note that found it. Until then `docs/getting-started/02-build-and-run.md`
tells a builder to rebuild the prefix whenever `sel4/config/` or the pins move. Reopen when
picking it up.
