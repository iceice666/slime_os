# One GiB of simultaneous isolated private working sets

## Context

Close the capacity epic with a simultaneously resident, isolated 1 GiB private working set, combining the scalable mechanism and a verified physical-memory envelope. Serial allocation/release of 256 MiB four times is not equivalent.

## Scope

- Raise the qualified QEMU envelopes through the target-bound Zutai budget path to 256 MiB per holder and 1024 MiB aggregate, building on the 64 MiB cutover. Preserve smaller targets and explicitly revalidate compatibility/version decisions.
- Carry the second ceiling raise through component-spec/system-spec validation and their negative fixtures, runtime probe bounds, and `docs/capability-matrix.md`; the earlier 64 MiB cutover does not automatically qualify the larger envelope.
- Extend the existing private-memory composition/probes with four holders and bounded synchronization over declared IPC. Keep all four 256 MiB private regions resident at once, then exercise holder death/restart while peers retain their data.
- Report reserved physical backing including task images, root/kernel objects, page tables, CSlots, and alignment overhead in addition to the 1 GiB of private payload. Fit the ARM 2 GiB profile and RV64 3 GiB profile without swap or overcommit.
- Before publishing the raised envelope, combine MEM-PLATFORM's measured post-exclusion ordinary-memory inventory with MEM-ARENAS' actual reservation plan. If ARM lacks the required payload plus all overhead, retain the previous admitted ceiling and record the resource shortfall; the final milestone and epic remain unclosed. Optimize in-scope backing or obtain an explicit revised platform/target decision through MyQue and the decision log. Never silently reduce the workload, drop the ARM lane, or count launcher RAM as free backing.
- Do not widen shared-buffer capacity or grant transfer authority over private memory to make the workload fit. ELF size, SMP, worker counts, 1 GiB frames, automatic compaction, live shrink, and physical-board qualification remain outside this epic.

## Exit conditions

- On both QEMU architectures, four live holders each grow exactly 256 MiB through the public private-memory path, verify initial zeroes, write distinct per-task/per-page patterns, and retain all data until a coordinator observes the full simultaneous 1024 MiB population. The aligned bulk case uses 128 large data frames per holder; account separately for non-data objects.
- At the holder and aggregate boundaries, an extra page or an over-declared generation is refused without changing accepted extents. One task cannot read, execute, share, or overwrite another's private bytes; reserved-window collision protection still holds.
- Mixed small/large growth and the runtime heap path remain correct within quota. Inject allocation/mapping/construction failure after partial progress and prove transactional recovery; include fragmented backing and near-limit CSlot/descriptor cases, not only pristine bulk growth.
- While three holders retain and recheck their patterns, the fourth faults, is reclaimed, and restarts at 256 MiB with zero-filled new memory. Repeat 20 coordinated cycles, then tear down all holders; reusable backing, free slots, mappings, and quota totals return to the same pre-workload baseline with no downward drift.
- Existing shared-buffer operations still work independently within their unchanged declared budget while private memory is full. Both architectures emit ordered, mutation-tested evidence from their own exact image/profile identities.

## Progress and current blocker (2026-09-13)

Remains **open**. The prerequisites are done and the feasibility question is
answered, but no residency evidence exists, so nothing here is qualified.

What is now in place, from MEM-64M and MEM-PLATFORM:

- Capacity is target-bound in `contracts/private-memory-budget/v1`, so raising the
  two QEMU rows to `regionPages = 65536` / `totalPages = 262144` is a contract edit
  plus regeneration — no parallel mechanism is needed.
- The runtime backing path segments into 2 MiB extents at every quota, so a 256 MiB
  holder does not reserve one 256 MiB extent.
- The descriptor tables are sized from the larger of the runtime aggregate and the
  planner's four-holder report, plus one static envelope per live task. The previous
  sizing was exactly four maximal holders and zero other tasks.

ARM arithmetic, from observed evidence rather than estimate: the root admits
`bytes=1584454000` of ordinary memory (`SLIME_ROOT ordinary ranges=34 ...
end=0xbf94c000`). Against that, 1024 MiB of payload plus ~2 MiB of private leaf
tables plus the 16 MiB root CNode and six tasks' static backing leaves roughly
480 MiB. **It fits.** The tighter resource is root CSlots: four 65536-page holders
need 264196 of the 524288 the ARM kernel provides, about 50%, before the staged
graph.

The blocker is not arithmetic and not a timeout. The one attempted boot refused the
fourth holder's spawn:

    SLIME_GRAPH spawn failed task=1 component=private-memory-1g-holder-d
        error=DestinationSlotsExhausted
    [private-memory-1g-probe] FAIL initial holder-d spawn status=5

with only three holders staged. That run predates the descriptor-table fix landed in
MEM-64M and must be repeated before it means anything; the transcript is
[`devlog/2026-09-13-mem-64m-target-qualified-capacity/one-gib-spawn-exhaustion.log`](https://git.justaslime.dev/iceice666/slime_os-history/src/commit/45ed1745907b2d0a13fdf70c8b34eb635bed5f23/devlog/2026-09-13-mem-64m-target-qualified-capacity/one-gib-spawn-exhaustion.log).

The composition and four-holder probe written during that attempt were **removed**
rather than left in the tree: a composition declaring 65536-page holders the contract
does not admit cannot build, and a half-wired plane is not a partial result. Rebuild

`contracts/lifecycle-policy/v1`'s `maxRestartAttempts` is still 16. The 20-cycle
requirement needs it raised; that raise belongs to this milestone's own change,
alongside the workload that exercises attempt 17, rather than landing ahead of it.

## Qualification interpretation (2026-09-17)

The operator confirmed these acceptance details before implementation:

- Baseline means conservation of usable capacity: compare the first-spawn
  baseline with final teardown, requiring live mappings, charged private pages,
  and task-owned CSlots to return to baseline. Count never-allocated ordinary
  backing plus immediately reusable backing together; report retained extents,
  descriptors, and parent-untyped anchor CSlots separately and prove their reuse
  with another allocation. Retained anchors are occupied, not free CSlots;
  their count must not grow across equivalent reclamation phases.
  Historical high-water marks need not return to zero. Identical reclamation
  phases must show no downward drift.
- Twenty coordinated cycles mean holder D's initial incarnation followed by
  twenty actual fault/reclaim/restart-admission/replacement sequences (21
  incarnations total). A/B/C remain resident and recheck their full patterns
  each time. Restart admissions 17 through 20 are exercised explicitly;
  clean-exit evidence is separate, and all four holders are finally torn down.
