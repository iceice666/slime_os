# A pool-relative private-memory window above 8 GiB is refused by the leaf-span table and is fatal at boot

## Context

Found by the 2026-09-24 post-closure stress audit of MEM-ADAPTIVE (source
reading; current rows are at most 4 GiB and are unaffected).

A pool-mode maximum is the admitted pool (`Ledger::pool_pages`, capped only by
`MAX_PAGES`). The window reservation goes through `acquire_leaf_spans`, which
refuses more than `MAX_WINDOW_SPANS = 4096` spans (8 GiB) in
`object_allocator.rs`; on the boot path that refusal is `fatal!`
(`graph_runtime.rs`). Past 256 GiB the child address ceiling refuses first.
Admission never checks a fixed maximum against either limit, although the
comment in `private_memory.rs` says it does, and
`docs/architecture/private-memory.md` promises "a window of the whole pool".

## Exit conditions

Admission and binding clamp or refuse a maximum against the real window
limits with a stated rule, no inventory size makes a pool-mode spawn fatal, and
the documentation states the actual limit.

## Verification

Gates: `just test_host`, `just test_sel4_root`, `just private_memory_adaptive_check`

## Closure evidence

Fix commit `c4e5287`. `private_memory::MAX_WINDOW_PAGES` (4096 spans x 512 pages = 8 GiB) is the stated limit; `window_pages` clamps a pool-relative maximum to it and refuses a fixed one, and `AdaptivePolicy::admit` fails closed with `SLIME_MEM FAIL adaptive maximum exceeds window` before anything is published. `docs/architecture/private-memory.md` states the limit. Regression test `a_maximum_beyond_one_window_is_clamped_when_pooled_and_refused_when_fixed` reserves the exact limit, refuses one span more, and checks a 64 GiB pool clamps. Limit: no QEMU row exceeds 8 GiB, so the clamp is observed on the host only; the 256 GiB address ceiling is above the clamp and unreachable.
