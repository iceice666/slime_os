# A failed revoke while unwinding a spawn quarantines the private-memory incarnation without enrolling it for retirement retry

## Context

Found by the 2026-09-24 post-closure stress audit of MEM-ADAPTIVE (source
reading; needs a kernel revoke failure).

`graph_runtime/services/spawn.rs` `release_child` retires the incarnation with
`revoked = false` when `tasks.reclaim` fails; the ledger marks it quarantined
and live, but the task is never inserted into `retirements` (only the exit and
fault paths in `services.rs` do), so nothing retries it. The entitlement's
charge is held forever and every later spawn of that subject is refused by
`bind_with_maximum` as a duplicate incarnation.

## Exit conditions

A spawn-unwind revoke failure is enrolled in the same retirement retry as the
exit and fault paths, observed by an injected-failure test that a later retry
refunds the incarnation and the subject can spawn again.

## Verification

Gates: `just test_sel4_root`, `just private_memory_adaptive_lifecycle_check`
