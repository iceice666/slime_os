# budget validation bounded each holder but never the aggregate

## Context

Resolved 2026-07-26.

`SharedBufferBudget::validate_against` checked each holder's quota against fixed kernel ceilings but never summed holders, so a budget could promise more holders their full per-holder ceiling than the global table could ever hold at once, degrading a declared quota into first-come-first-served.

## Exit conditions

`validate_against` now sums `byte_pages`, `buffer_count`, `mapping_count`, and `loan_count` with saturating adds and rejects any total past the kernel ceiling, plus adds the missing per-holder mapping/loan bounds; `cargo test -p boot-contracts --lib` passes 24 tests including the new aggregate and per-holder cases, and raising a real manifest past the aggregate ceiling fails the boot closed.
