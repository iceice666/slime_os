# H1V1 PWM bench probe and pad qualification

## Context

Which SoC registers produce servo PWM, and which pad a channel reaches, are answerable from the
vendor BSP; which pad reaches a *connector* is not answerable from any source on the development
host. A bench probe from the vendor U-Boot prompt settles that, and pins the pinmux and clock
state Slime's own bring-up would otherwise have to assume.

The probe drives the clock generator and pinmux blocks, both of which are shared with PWM
channel 12 -- this SoC's CPU core-voltage regulator, programmed by U-Boot at boot. Every write
to a shared word is read-modify-write, and four core-rail readings are asserted before the first
write and after the last.

## Exit conditions

On the named Novatek NT98690 H1V1, from its unmodified vendor U-Boot: one PWM channel takes the
programmed free-run, period, and extended-period values and reports them back; a servo or ESC on
the identified header pin follows 1000, 1500, and 2000 us pulse widths at 50 Hz; the probe
restores every register it changed; and the vendor firmware banner returns after `reset` and
after a power cycle. The channel, pad, header pin, and the observed pinmux state are pinned in
`sel4/pins.toml`.

## Verification

Gates: `python3 scripts/check/check-nt98690-boot.py --pwm-probe --serial <tty>` and
`--gpio-probe <channel>` (bench modes, no `just` recipe), `just sel4_pin_check`,
`just sel4_gate_control_check`

## Evidence

Observed on 2026-09-08 on the named board, from the vendor U-Boot prompt: PWM0 reaches
P_GPIO[0] on the 40-pin GPIO header; the pinmux word held no routing and every P_GPIO was in
GPIO mode at handoff; channel 0 took the programmed clock, free-run, period, and extended-period
values and read them back with channel 12 untouched; a drone ESC armed at 1000 us and ran faster
at 1600 than at 1200 (operator observation); the GPIO data register never reflected the running
pad. The run was interrupted before its closing disable and reset, so the restore-and-banner
tail is still unobserved; one clean run closes this item.
