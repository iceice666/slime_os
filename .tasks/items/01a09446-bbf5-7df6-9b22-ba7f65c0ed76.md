# network-service cannot observe a client that dies without the shutdown rendezvous while it holds the pages that client lent

## Context

`network-service` maps the queue page and data pages each client lends it (IO11). A client
that exits without the shutdown rendezvous — every `fail()` in `io-tcp-probe` is `exit(1)`
with no `OP_CLOSE` — is invisible to the service: a native endpoint has no closed-peer
signal (`slime-root/src/ipc.rs`, `IpcError` has no `PeerDead` by design), death travels only
on a supervision capability, and no mechanism places a supervision capability between two
root-launched instances (`mintedBindings` are supplied by the owner that spawns a child, and
these instances are launched by the root). The root reclaims the dead client's loans and
removes the service's mappings of them, so the service's next poll of that client's queue
page faults, taking the link and every other client down. Observed once during IO11's
second stream boot, in the shape the shutdown rendezvous then closed for the orderly exit;
the disorderly exit remains open.

## Exit conditions

A root-launched service can learn that a root-launched client died before it touches a page
that client lent: either a `supervision` grant the generation declares between two launched
instances and the root places at launch, or a liveness query on a declared endpoint. With it,
`network-service` drops a dead client's queue without touching it, aborts its sockets, and
keeps serving the others; a plane observes a client compiled to die mid-stream and the
service ending healthy.

## Verification

Gates: a `sel4-io-tcp` death arm in `just io_tcp_check` once the mechanism exists;
`just test_sel4_root` for the root side.

## Disposition

Deferred 2026-09-12 by decision: the fix is a root mechanism (a supervision capability the
generation can place between two root-launched instances, or an endpoint liveness query),
which is its own milestone rather than a follow-up to IO11. The service's orderly exit is
already closed by the shutdown rendezvous; this item is the disorderly one. Reopen when the
mechanism lands or when a plane observes the fault.
