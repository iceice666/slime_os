# MAVLink heartbeat on the H1V1

## Context

The board half of the lane: the root carves and brings up UART8, the build inputs carry the
board's clock and divisor from the pins, and a gate observes the result on two serial ports at
once -- the board's console for its markers, and a ground radio for what actually reached the
air.

## Exit conditions

On the named Novatek NT98690 H1V1, from one power-on: after `SLIME_ROOT READY`, the console
shows the driver's ready marker naming the clock and divisor it read back, and the producer's
per-send marker with a consecutive sequence and an `ok` status; the ground radio's port decodes
at least ten consecutive MAVLink v2 heartbeats with valid checksums, sequence numbers
consecutive modulo 256, and inter-arrival times within 1 s +/- 250 ms; no failure marker
appears; the gate-only terminator returns the board to its vendor firmware; zero framing errors
on the console.

No operator observation appears in this exit condition. The actuator here is a receiver the
checker owns, so unlike `P6.D` the whole claim is carried by the two transcripts.
