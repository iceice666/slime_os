# a `retained` second route on one publisher stops a *different* publisher's parked role reply from ever being taken

## Context

Resolved 2026-08-07.

On the P5.4.5 QoS plane, `fabric-publisher` parked once in `recv` awaiting its role reply and never ran again, although both role capabilities were delivered to it.

## Exit conditions

The cause was `MAX_GRAPH_ITERATIONS = 512` — the QoS plane needs more than 512 and fewer than 768 root round-trips — not a lost wake, stale capability, or scheduler defect. Bound raised to 2048; `just sel4_qos_check` passed fourteen markers across nine causal chains, and restoring 512 reproduces the `wedged waiter` signature.
