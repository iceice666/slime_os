# all child execution shares one fixed priority and no scheduling authority

## Context

Resolved 2026-08-12, MCS-only clauses explicitly deferred.

every child ran at one fixed priority and the generation's schedule records had no effect on running TCBs; MCS-only budget/period/donation/ timeout-fault features were also unavailable on the selected AArch64 kernel configuration.

## Exit conditions

priority is authenticated per-thread generation data, bounded at 254, and applied to boot and spawn TCBs, observed by a worker at lower priority spinning without starving its main thread. MCS stays deliberately off — upstream AArch64 MCS proofs are in progress — with the decision and revisit condition recorded separately. `just sel4_qos_check`, `just sel4_sample_check`, and the platform-timer assertions in `just sel4_root_boot_check` pass; `just devlog_check` passes with the assurance decision indexed.
