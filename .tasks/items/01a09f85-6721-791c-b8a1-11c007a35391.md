# Separate current product knowledge from development history

## Problem

The product repository currently combines the source and verification needed to
maintain Slime OS with 327 chronological development records, a frozen backlog
index retained for those records, and raw investigation artifacts. Active
policy also requires a new devlog for ordinary non-trivial changes. This makes
historical compatibility a permanent product concern and forces current design
knowledge to be recovered by date rather than by owning subsystem.

Separate current product knowledge from development history without changing
runtime behavior, ABI, hardware qualification, work-item identity or state, or
the bytes and meaning of existing evidence.

## Scope and ownership

- `.tasks/items/` remains the only live store for problem, scope, state, exit
  conditions, hierarchy, and dependencies.
- The product repository retains implementation, contracts, generators,
  checkers, recipes, necessary fixtures and observation records, current
  architecture and operating documentation, accepted decisions, plans, and
  exploratory directions.
- A separate history repository, provisionally `slime_os-history`, preserves
  historical investigations, delivery narrative, superseded architecture, and
  safely archived raw artifacts. It must not become a build, test, documentation,
  submodule, or network dependency of the product repository.
- Large raw logs, transcripts, and captures stop entering the product tree by
  default. Checker inputs and compact observation records remain with the
  checker that consumes them.
- Existing evidence bytes, digests, observation times, tested image identities,
  and pass/fail/unbound status remain unchanged. An archive is not a new
  qualification result.

## Staged implementation

Implementation is four independently reviewable changes. Each child starts
from current `main` only after its dependency is merged; implementation changes
must not be stacked on this planning change or on another unmerged child.

1. `01a09f86-10dc-73fd-8b6e-d127999945d0` — remove the mandatory ordinary
   devlog policy while preserving evidence-based completion.
2. `01a09f86-348d-7294-9e1e-0102d7f03e3e` — extract current architecture,
   plans, decisions, and reusable investigation guidance into owning docs.
3. `01a09f86-5bd7-7ac0-bd66-35cae6fc2982` — establish and verify the history
   archive, then atomically cut product consumers over and remove only archived
   historical material.
4. `01a09f86-802e-70bb-9dfe-87d97822802c` — audit and retire history-only
   recipe aliases and migration scaffolding after the cutover has landed.

## Unresolved archive prerequisites

- No authorized `slime_os-history` remote or retention policy has been
  established by this planning change.
- No repository-independent artifact service was identified for large raw
  captures. Existing material therefore remains in the source tree until a
  verifiable archive or bundle exists.
- H3 must inventory reachable Git history, submodule/LFS/external attachments,
  old commit references, permissions, credentials/privacy exposure, broken
  historical links, and backup/publication status. A full tree snapshot is not
  by itself a claim that every external historical dependency is recoverable.
- Without an authorized remote, H3 may produce and verify a local bundle and
  restoration instructions, but deletion from the product repository remains
  blocked.

## Exit conditions

1. The four child changes land in dependency order from fresh `main`, with no
   stacked implementation PRs.
2. Ordinary feature and bug-fix work needs no separate devlog, but PRs and work
   items still record claims, risk, exact verification, and observed exit
   conditions.
3. Current architecture, plans, decisions, and exploration are directly
   navigable in owning product documentation and are source-backed.
4. Historical records and raw bytes removed from the product tree are
   independently restorable and digest-verifiable at a named full commit/tree.
5. General product development, documentation checks, and host tests neither
   require nor contact the history repository.
6. `.tasks/items/` identity, state, hierarchy, dependencies, and backlog-first
   enforcement remain canonical and valid without a frozen backlog document.
7. Checker fixtures and hardware observation records required for current
   qualifications remain in the product tree; missing, forged, mismatched, or
   unbound evidence continues to fail closed.
8. Runtime code, contracts, ABI, build pins, image identities, signing keys,
   runner trust, branch protection, and hardware support claims are unchanged.
9. History-only aliases and obsolete chronological-index/template obligations
   are removed only after their consumers are proven absent or migrated.
10. Final verification covers current local links/fragments/UUIDs,
    `just tasks_check`, `just typos`, Python lint and owning checker controls,
    CI syntax, affected recipe aggregates, archive mismatch refusal, and the
    existing physical-evidence refusal controls.

## Non-goals

Do not split the Rust workspace, components, contracts, SDK, or monorepo; change
runtime or ABI semantics; rewrite product Git history; create a new tracker,
documentation platform, or artifact service; migrate GitHub projection or SDK
publication; change release keys or forge protection; move the Framework CPU
observation out of the product tree; or reinterpret historical evidence as
proof of the current image.
