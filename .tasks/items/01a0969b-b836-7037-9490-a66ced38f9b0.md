# network-service learns its clients from its bindings, not from a compiled-in grant table

## Context

`network-service` resolves its clients through a compiled-in table (`CLIENTS` in
`components/services/network-service/src/main.rs`): the grant name each client's endpoint
resolves by and the instance name its holder identity derives from. `docs/syscall-abi.md`
states that grant names are not stable across generations and cannot be written into a
component, and `kind:endpoint` resolution refuses a holder with several endpoint bindings, so
a service with several clients has no other route today. Every new client of the service
(the board lane's first application, any later one) edits the service and re-blesses its
closure. Raised by the maintainability review of PR #32 (IO11), 2026-09-12.

## Exit conditions

A self-scoped root query answers a component's own endpoint bindings with each peer's
instance identity (the root already resolves a slot to its peer instance for
`CAPABILITY IMPORT`, `peer_instance_for_slot` in `slime-root/src/generation.rs`), or an
equivalent declared mechanism. `CLIENTS` is gone: the service enumerates its clients from
that answer, and a generation adds a client to the service without a service change. The
`sel4-io-network` and `sel4-io-tcp` planes pass unchanged; a composition that binds a client
by a new grant name reaches the service without a rebuild.

## Verification

Gates: `just test_sel4_root` for the query; `just io_network_check` and `just io_tcp_check`
for the service; a composition case whose client grant name the service has never seen.

## Disposition

Deferred 2026-09-12 by decision: the fix is a root mechanism, its own change rather than a
follow-up to IO11. P6.F (Ethernet on the H1V1) depends on this item, so the next client lane
lands it first.
