# Adopting a split child extent for infrastructure permanently prevents its buddy pair from merging

## Context

Found by the 2026-09-24 post-closure stress audit of MEM-ADAPTIVE (allocator
harness `build/mem-adaptive-stress2/allocator/`).

`adopt_returned_extent` picks the smallest common-free extent that fits, usually
a split child; once adopted it is active for the root's lifetime, so its parent
can never merge and its free sibling never re-forms the larger block. Host
harness: after adopting one 1 MiB child of a split 2 MiB extent, coalescing does
nothing and a 2 MiB request returns `NoKernelUntyped`. In
`build/sel4-private-memory-matrix-mem1024.log` four of six adoptions are split
children (e.g. adopted 26607 beside free sibling 26606).

## Exit conditions

Adoption prefers candidates that do not strand a free buddy (or the inventory
reports stranded capacity honestly), with a host regression covering the
split-then-adopt sequence.

## Verification

Gates: `just test_sel4_root`

## Closure evidence

Fix commit `1036a06`. Adoption ranks a split child one size above a whole extent, so it takes an unsplit extent when one fits and does not pin a free buddy.
