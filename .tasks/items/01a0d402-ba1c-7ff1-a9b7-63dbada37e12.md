# Adaptive ledger does not price root metadata growth, so the operational reserve is consumed at pool exhaustion

## Context

Found by the 2026-09-24 post-closure stress audit of MEM-ADAPTIVE, from the
existing matrix transcripts `build/sel4-private-memory-matrix{,-rv64}-mem*.log`.

Root metadata (`infrastructure_owned`) grows during the all-small and mixed
walks (arm 2 GiB +14.3 MB, arm 4 GiB +68.9 MB, rv64 3 GiB +51.3 MB, rv64 4 GiB
+82.8 MB) but elastic transactions price only extents, descriptors and slots,
never the metadata pages that back them. At the final one-page refusals the
allocator's elastic inventory was 311 KB / 90 KB / 109 KB / 113 KB against a
16 MiB operational reserve, while the ledger still reported 35-67 MB free and
the refusal cause became `ordinary-layout` rather than `ledger-pool`. The
"reserve funds a spawn" check runs only after the bulk walk, and the matrix
residual check at `check-sel4-private-memory-plane.py` enforces only an upper
bound.

## Exit conditions

Either the ledger charges the metadata a growth creates, or the reserve is
sized and enforced against it; at every exhaustion point on every matrix row
the allocator residual is at least the operational reserve, a construction
under exhaustion after the mixed walk succeeds, and the matrix plane asserts
both.

## Verification

Gates: `just private_memory_matrix_check`, `just private_memory_phase5_check`
