# Adaptive ledger does not price root metadata growth, so the operational reserve is consumed at pool exhaustion

## Context

Found by the 2026-09-24 post-closure stress audit of MEM-ADAPTIVE, from the
existing matrix transcripts `build/sel4-private-memory-matrix{,-rv64}-mem*.log`.

Root metadata (`infrastructure_owned`) grows during the all-small and mixed
walks (arm 2 GiB +14.3 MB, arm 4 GiB +68.9 MB, rv64 3 GiB +51.3 MB, rv64 4 GiB
+82.8 MB) but elastic transactions price only extents, descriptors and slots,
never the metadata pages that back them. At the final one-page refusals the
allocator's elastic inventory was 311 KB / 90 KB / 109 KB / 113 KB against a
16 MiB operational reserve, while the ledger still reported 35-67 MB free and
the refusal cause became `ordinary-layout` rather than `ledger-pool`. The
"reserve funds a spawn" check runs only after the bulk walk, and the matrix
residual check at `check-sel4-private-memory-plane.py` enforces only an upper
bound.

## Exit conditions

Either the ledger charges the metadata a growth creates, or the reserve is
sized and enforced against it; at every exhaustion point on every matrix row
the allocator residual is at least the operational reserve, a construction
under exhaustion after the mixed walk succeeds, and the matrix plane asserts
both.

## Verification

Gates: `just private_memory_matrix_check`, `just private_memory_phase5_check`

## Deferral

Deferred on 2026-09-25 by decision, while its sibling stress-audit defects were
fixed on `fix/mem-adaptive-stress-audit`. Closing it needs a policy choice this
defect cannot make alone: either every elastic demand prices the metadata pages
its records and directory growth will consume (a ledger resource change that
touches admission, certification and every checker row), or the operational
reserve is sized from the inventory (metadata grew 0.9-1.95 % of the pool) and
the allocator refuses ordinary growth that would enter it. The two differ in
who owns the cost and in how the matrix rows' exhaustion points move, so the
choice belongs to a spec-driven follow-up rather than to a backlog fix. No
data is lost meanwhile: the refusal is fail-closed, and holders that exit
return the capacity.

## Closure evidence

which took the first option: root infrastructure adoption and system backing
are charged to the ledger before any effect, and the allocator enforces the
operational reserve as a floor. The original implementation and observations
were recorded on branch `fix/adaptive-metadata-reserve` at commit `628acfa`,
QEMU only, matrix closures
`sel4-private-memory-matrix@6a14175a27bb` and
`sel4-private-memory-matrix-rv64@ec8c4b97d6d6`, 2026-09-25/26:

  (qemu-arm-virt 1024/2048/4096 MiB, qemu-riscv-virt 1024/3072/4096 MiB)
  names `ledger-pool`. Allocator residual at those refusals was 16781136 bytes
  on every ARM row and 16779728-16779760 bytes on RV64, against the declared
  16777216-byte reserve; the checker now fails a residual below the reserve.

  mixed-pressure holders stayed resident (`mixed probe complete ...
