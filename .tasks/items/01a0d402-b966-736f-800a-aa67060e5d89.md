# A guaranteed growth that fails after ledger begin never restores the descriptors and CSlots it lent from its reservation

## Context

Found by the 2026-09-24 post-closure stress audit of MEM-ADAPTIVE.

`slime-root/src/private_memory/elastic.rs` `grow` lends
`pages + tables` descriptors and CSlots from the entitlement's reservation
(`lend_reserved_resources`) before `ledger.begin`. The two failure arms after
`begin` (commit-private-transaction failure and `map_growth` failure) call only
`settle_failure`, which releases the pooled acquisition and aborts the ledger,
but never calls `restore_reserved_resources` for `lent` nor releases the
guaranteed backing plan. `ledger.abort` does return the pending guarantee to
`guarantee_free`, and `AdaptivePolicy::retire` restores only the committed
`guaranteed_held`, so the aborted lend is never returned.

Consequence: each such failure moves the lent counts from the reservation to
common capacity while the ledger still reports the guarantee redeemable; after
a few failures `lend_reserved_resources` is `Unavailable` and every guaranteed
growth in the cohort is refused. Item
`01a0d402-b90b-7984-95ee-d891b34d4e10` makes such a failure
component-triggerable. `task.rs`
`grow_private_memory_adaptive` carries a dead `let before = …; let _ = before;`
where a restore check was evidently intended.

## Exit conditions

Every post-begin failure of a guaranteed growth restores exactly the funding it
lent and returns its borrowed backing, observed by a slime-root host regression
that drives the production `grow` through a failing mapping and compares the
reservation's descriptors and CSlots before and after; adaptive planes pass.

## Verification

Gates: `just test_sel4_root`, `just private_memory_adaptive_check`,
`just private_memory_adaptive_lifecycle_check`
