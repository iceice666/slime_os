# sel4-rollback plane: virtio-blk-driver faults after its client is reclaimed (intermittent; CI run 34474105011 on 65443ce0)

## Context

Upstream CI run 34474105011 (the push of `65443ce0`, PR #26's merge) failed the job
"Rollback, release trust, and BootState trace" at `just bootstate_trace_check` ->
`sel4_rollback_check`: the rollback probe printed `rollback plane complete` and exited before
the driver had, the root reclaimed it (`SLIME_GRAPH holder reclaimed task=3 charges=6
actions=36`, two charges more than a healthy run because the driver still held its two
loans), and the required driver instance then faulted:

    SLIME_ROOT FATAL SLIME_GRAPH FAIL required instance virtio-blk-driver fault
    kind=VirtualMemory { access: Execute, status: 64 } instruction=Some(1527) address=Some(64)

The same tree passed that job five other times in CI and fifteen times locally. Widening the
driver's window between receiving the shutdown command and its final drain reproduced the
CI transcript and the fault on every boot: `BlockIo::shutdown` was a blocking send that
completed the moment the polling driver received it, so nothing kept the client from
exiting, and being reclaimed, while the driver still drained the ring the client had lent
it. The shutdown is now a call the driver answers only after that drain, and it prints its
exit marker before replying so the plane checkers' ordered chains no longer depend on the
scheduler.

Record: `devlog/2026-09-11-b93-rollback-driver-fault/`.

## Exit conditions

Observed 2026-09-11: with the shutdown rendezvous in place and the driver's window still
widened, two checker boots and two 15 s direct boots of the rollback plane ran clean where
the unfixed image faulted on every boot; on the final source, `just sel4_rollback_check`,
`just io_block_check`, `just sel4_storage_check`, `just sel4_store_check`, `just
replay_check`, `just sel4_generation_check`, `just sel4_filesystem_check`, `just
sel4_recovery_plane_check`, `just sel4_transfer_check`, and `just bootstate_trace_check`
all passed. The first CI run of the rollback job on this change is the broader observation
the devlog entry waits on under `Monitoring`.

## Verification
