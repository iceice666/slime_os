Two slime-root bounds raised for the C8.10 full-graph boot
Date: 2026-08-08

Both were sized against a single-plane graph and both are the same class as
B28's MAX_GRAPH_ITERATIONS: a table sized to a workload rather than to an
invariant. Neither is a capability-model change.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. channel::MAX_CHANNELS  32 -> 48
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Needed: 37 live at the peak.
  16 participant control channels (init mints one per participant)
   3 call role channels
   4 operation role channels
  14 stream role channels (two per stream edge: data + credit/ack, over
     seven declared edges)

The previous bound was `task::MAX_TASKS`, chosen in P5.5.2 on the reasoning
that growth is driven by route roles rather than task pairs. That is right and
still leaves the number wrong: a full graph running every plane at once needs
more roles than a graph running one.

Failure shape if left at 32: the fabric's 33rd endpoint_create is refused, and
every participant downstream of it fails too, which reads as four broken
components rather than one exhausted table. That is the same symptom P5.5.2
recorded at 16.

Cost: ~4.2 KiB per entry (two queues of CHANNEL_CAPACITY x MAX_MESSAGE_BYTES),
so ~66 KiB of additional .bss.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. task::MAX_TASKS  32 -> 48
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Needed: 37 live tasks.

This is the number the pre-slice analysis got wrong, and the reason is a real
difference between the two roots rather than a miscount. The oracle boots 20
tasks. On slime-root the same generation is 37:

  20  the root launches every component the generation declares (P5.2)
+ 17  init spawns the fabric and all 16 participants again, because a spawned
      child is the only one holding a control endpoint init minted
= 37

The 20 root-launched instances are the unconfigured copies every seL4 plane
already carries; here there are twenty of them rather than five.

Failure shape if left at 32: serve_spawn answers DestinationSlotsExhausted at
the 13th participant and init reports a spawn failure — a composition defect,
not an exhausted table.

Cost: GraphTables holds MAX_GRAPH_TASKS (== MAX_TASKS) capability tables of
MAX_TASK_CAPS entries, ~1.5 KiB each, so ~24 KiB of additional .bss.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Why 48 and not 37
--------------------------------------------------------------------

B28's rule. A bound raised to the first passing number is a bound that moves
again at the next graph, and neither of these fails cleanly — each produces a
cascade that reads as a composition defect. 48 is one headroom step, and both
tables live in statics for backlog B3's reason.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Two gates caught the consequences, which is the point of having them
--------------------------------------------------------------------

just sel4_crossing_check:
  "the crossing loop mints 33 pairs against MAX_CHANNELS=48; it must exceed the
   bound or the gate proves nothing"
  The gate reads BOTH constants from source and refuses pairs <= bound. Raising
  MAX_CHANNELS alone would have left a gate that still passes while proving
  nothing. CHANNEL_LOOP_PAIRS moved 33 -> 49.

just sel4_root_boot_check:
  "missing marker: ... slots=832..882"
  Observed 839..889 and 889..939. The absolute base is wherever the allocator's
  cursor stands once the root's static tables are placed, so larger tables moved
  it by 7. The property — each range 50 wide, the second adjoining the first
  exactly — is unchanged, and the pins were updated to the observed base with
  that distinction recorded at the assertion.
