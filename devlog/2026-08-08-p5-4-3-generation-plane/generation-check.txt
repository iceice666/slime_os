Two defects found while bringing this up, both recorded in the entry:
1. The manager's authority probe consumed the client's first request. A probing
   `recv` on a slot whose peer sends real traffic is not the same operation as a
   probing `recv` on a run token.

   copies of both queue ends. `peer_alive` is a property of who still names a
   queue end.
